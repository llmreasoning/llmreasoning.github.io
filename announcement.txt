## 👋 Welcome to the LLM Reasoning Tutorial community!

This space is for everyone following our tutorial on **Current Advances in LLM Reasoning** — presented across **ICML 2026 (Seoul)**, **ACL 2026 (San Diego)**, and **IJCAI 2026 (Bremen)**, including the ICML edition, *Adaptive Reasoning in LLMs: From Post-Training to Test-Time Learning*.

We’re using Discussions as a place to connect with other members of our community. We hope that you:
  * Ask questions you’re wondering about — about the talks, the slides, the hands-on notebooks, or reasoning in LLMs more broadly.
  * Share ideas, papers, and experiments.
  * Engage with other community members, whether you’re a researcher or a practitioner.
  * Welcome others and are open-minded. Remember that this is a community we build together 💪.

A few good places to start:
  * 🌐 Tutorial site: https://llmreasoning.github.io
  * 📓 Hands-on materials: https://github.com/au-clan/llmreasoning_materials
  * 🙋 Attending one of the venues? Tell us which one and say hi.

To get started, comment below with an introduction of yourself and tell us what brings you to LLM reasoning.
